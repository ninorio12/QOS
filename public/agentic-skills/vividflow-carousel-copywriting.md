# VividFlow Carousel Copywriting

## Core Principle

A VividFlow carousel is not a mini-article. It is a sequence of decisions: stop, recognize the pain, feel the cost, understand the mechanism, click.

## Slide Logic

Default 5-slide structure:
1. Hook: one sharp operational truth.
2. Recognition: the agency owner sees their current workflow.
3. Cost: what this silently burns.
4. VividFlow mechanism: how the agentic layer fixes the workflow.
5. CTA: one action, no clutter.

For 7 slides, add:
- One slide for false belief.
- One slide for before/after workflow.

## Hook Rules

Good hooks:
- Short.
- Concrete.
- Slightly uncomfortable.
- Specific to real-estate agency operations.

Bad hooks:
- “Découvrez comment...”
- “L'IA révolutionne...”
- “Le futur de l'immobilier...”
- Any claim that could fit a generic CRM.

## Density Rules

- One idea per slide.
- 3 lines max for hero copy where possible.
- Every word must move the reader forward.
- Avoid vague nouns: solution, innovation, transformation, synergie.
- Do not use em-dashes.

## Voice

Public voice:
- VividFlow speaks as a company.
- Formal “vous”.
- No Jonathan, no Thomas.
- No human signature.
- French from France.

## CTA Rules

CTA must match intent:
- TOFU: “Voir comment une agence peut reprendre le contrôle de ses opérations.”
- MOFU: “Demander une démonstration.”
- BOFU: “Réserver un échange.”
- Use Kalengi/City-to-City only if that is the current live destination.

## Quality Gate

Reject copy if:
- The first slide does not stop the scroll in under 2 seconds.
- The problem is not visibly Swiss real-estate agency specific.
- The cost is abstract.
- The solution says “IA” without describing the workflow.
- The CTA feels bolted on.
