# VividFlow Typography Calibration

## Core Principle

Typography is the main visual object in VividFlow posts. It must feel composed, not merely styled.

## Required Moves

- Use heavy rounded grotesk feeling: compact, confident, premium. Avoid default web-template rhythm.
- Headline must be sculpted into 2 to 4 intentional lines.
- Use tight but readable line-height. Never let capsules collide with adjacent lines.
- Make one word the hero object: capsule, sticker, black bar, selection, strike-through, or boxed correction.
- Capsule padding must be generous and optically balanced, especially after punctuation.
- Body copy must be quieter, shorter, and more contrasted than previous drafts.

## Checks Before Rendering

- Is the headline readable in under 2 seconds?
- Does the line break create meaning?
- Is the word pivot visually dominant?
- Does the capsule look designed rather than CSS default?
- Does the body text support the headline without competing?

## Reject If

- The slide feels like a landing page hero section.
- The type looks like default Poppins/Manrope web UI.
- The word pivot is just colored text.
- The capsule is too tight, too flat, or mechanically aligned.
- The headline could be pasted into any SaaS template.
