# VividFlow Carousel Reverse Engineering

## Core Principle

Do not copy the surface. Extract the operating logic: message structure, typographic hierarchy, visual metaphor, motion between slides, and conversion role.

## Analysis Order

1. **Message logic**
   - What is the single truth of the slide?
   - What opposition is used? Examples: volume vs intelligence, canal vs méthode, hasard vs système.
   - Which word carries the idea?

2. **Typographic logic**
   - Identify H1, body, micro-copy, labels, CTA.
   - Locate the word turned into an object: capsule, selection, black bar, sticker, underline, strike-through.
   - Note line breaks and why they isolate meaning.

3. **Scene logic**
   - What concrete object proves the idea? Phone, key, card, dashboard, message, CRM card, person.
   - Is the scene human pain, system control, money loss, or operational clarity?

4. **Depth and background**
   - Orange = action, heat, acquisition, urgency, money.
   - Blue/black = precision, IA, control, system, premium.
   - Photo = pain, human context, concrete situation.
   - UI blur = proof of system without cognitive overload.

5. **Brand system**
   - Check logo top center on covers.
   - Check brand pill bottom-left on carousel slides.
   - Check chevron/CTA bottom-right.
   - Check if VividFlow is recognizable without reading the logo.

6. **Slide-to-slide rhythm**
   - Stable components: palette, typo, capsules, brand pill, chevron.
   - Variable components: object, scene, text position, visual proof.
   - A carousel must feel like a sequence of scenes, not a stack of documents.

## Source-Recovery Trigger

If Jonathan or Thomas says to “look at the images Jonathan gave” or references a date/time rather than sending the images again, do not ask for a resend by default. Search prior sessions and local caches for the referenced image/base64/document paths, especially Figma exports and ZIPs. For the 13 May VividFlow calibration, the key source pattern came from `Samedi.png` plus `Logo.svg` and `Profil Button.svg`: orange/red full-bleed, small top logo, short headline, black word-pivot sticker, central proof card, and realistic mobile/person/object at the bottom.

## Output Format

Return:
- Core creative formula.
- Copywriting patterns.
- Typography patterns.
- Layout and spacing rules.
- Brand component rules.
- Visual metaphor library.
- Mistakes to avoid next time.
- Production checklist.

## Common Mistakes

- Saying only “premium, black/orange, glassmorphism”. That is surface-level.
- Ignoring the word pivot.
- Treating UI cards as decoration instead of proof.
- Missing the bottom-left brand pill and bottom-right navigation system.
- Producing a clean document instead of a memorable scene.
- Copying the graphic style without preserving the contradiction in the copy.
